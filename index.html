myemailmanager

A personal command-line mail tool.

What it does

myemailmanager reads and sends mail from its own author's Gmail mailbox over IMAP and SMTP. It runs locally on the author's machine as a command-line program. It is not offered as a hosted service and has no users other than the author.

Google account access

The tool authenticates with Google using OAuth 2.0. It requests the https://mail.google.com/ scope, which is what IMAP and SMTP access to a Gmail mailbox requires. Access is granted explicitly by the account owner on Google's consent screen and can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account permissions page.

Where data goes

Nowhere. Messages and OAuth tokens stay on the local machine running the tool. No data is sent to the author, to a server, or to any third party.
